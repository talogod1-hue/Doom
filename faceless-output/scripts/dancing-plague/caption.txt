In July 1518, a woman named Frau Troffea started dancing in a Strasbourg street, and she didn't stop for days.

Within a week, dozens had joined her. By late summer, hundreds were dancing through the city, and many collapsed. Some died of heart attacks, strokes, or exhaustion.

Town officials hired musicians and built a dance hall, hoping the crowd could dance it out. It didn't work. Historians still argue about the cause.
