The Last Sunrise on Server Eight
Jesse had spent three hundred days building the Sunrise Tower, and tonight his best friend Mika was going to tear it down.
Mika had never forgiven Jesse for taking the diamond vein at the spawn ridge. Their old base had been there for two years, and one night it was simply gone, with a sign that said "Claimed by Jesse."
Jesse tried to reach him on the server chat, but Mika had muted him. So Jesse did what he always did when he was scared. He mined.
He dug down past bedrock into the cold dark caves below the world, following the echo of a creeper he had heard the night before. At the bottom, he found a chest with a note in Mika's handwriting: "I didn't take the vein. I gave it to you. You never asked why I was leaving."
Jesse climbed back up with the sun already rising. The Sunrise Tower was glowing, and at its base stood Mika, holding a bucket of water and a bundle of wheat.
"We moved to the mountains," Mika said. "You never looked."
Jesse put down his pickaxe. For the first time in weeks, he did not want to build anything at all.
